# Risk indicators — contextual, not conclusive

| Signal | Why it matters | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory paid course, certificate or registration to secure a job | Advance-fee recruiting is a common scam pathway | Distinguish employer-funded onboarding from candidate-funded conditions |
| Request for passwords, OTPs, bank login or remote-access installation | Direct account compromise / device compromise risk | Treat as urgent protective action |
| Promise of guaranteed hire without normal screening | Unusual and creates pressure | Recruitment models vary |
| Unsolicited request for identification or banking details in early stages | Identity/privacy exposure | Employers may legitimately collect some data later through secure channels |
| Switch to unverified messenger or a different identity | Weakens attribution to the claimed employer | Legitimate recruiters use messaging too |
| Free email account claiming to be a major brand | Harder to authenticate | Small businesses and third-party recruiters can use consumer email |
| Contradictory location, compensation, employer, job title or remote policy | Suggests template reuse or deception | Request clarification before a definitive conclusion |
| Urgency, threats, secrecy or pressure to act before verification | Social engineering | Some genuine processes have deadlines |
| Distinctive script found under different employer identities | Strong corroboration of impersonation patterns | Confirm the sources and dates; copies can circulate innocently |

**Critical action trigger:** a demand for an OTP, credential, payment to release promised employment, or installing remote-control software warrants immediate protective guidance even if perpetrator attribution remains uncertain.
